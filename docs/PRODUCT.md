# VIDEO-TO.PRO

## Register
product

## Users
Content creators: bloggers, SMM managers, TikTok/Reels/YouTube Shorts creators. They convert video files for social media daily. Non-technical, want results in seconds. Using the tool on laptops and desktops, usually during content production sessions.

## Product Purpose
Client-side video conversion tool. No uploads to server, everything runs in the browser via FFmpeg WASM. Seven converters: Video to GIF, MOV to MP4, IMG to JPG, Video to JPG, Video Cutter, Remove HDR, Sequential Batch.

## Brand Personality
Confident tool. Neobrutalism aesthetic: bold structure (thick borders, hard shadows, sharp corners, uppercase headings) but restrained color intensity. Not punk, not aggressive. Think Linear meets Gumroad: professional, opinionated, memorable.

## Anti-references
- Apple minimalism: no thin lines, excessive white space, gray everything
- Corporate SaaS: no blue gradients, white cards, stock photos
- Acid 90s: no maximum-saturation colors that hurt readability

## Strategic Design Principles
1. Tool-first: every visual decision serves usability, not decoration
2. Instant recognition: neobrutalism structure makes the brand memorable without screaming
3. Readable always: color contrast must pass WCAG AA minimum, even with bold palette choices
4. Speed perception: the interface should feel fast and responsive through motion and layout
